We at the Barangay Batasan Hills are committed to help foster our local vendors and provide benefits that will help bloom their business.

Security and Stall Support

The registered vendors obtain legal rights which protect their designated areas. The vendors receive access to ₱5,000 to ₱8,000 livelihood kits which they can use for stall renovations and market stall improvements

Better Financial Options

The official registration process allows businesses to obtain government-funded loans that include DTI Negosyo Fund which provides lower interest rates compared to "5-6" lenders. The financial programs enable businesses to buy additional inventory without facing excessive financial obligations.

Business Growth and Digital Tools

Vendors can attend free professional workshops and seminars which help them to develop their business skills. The company provides vendors with digital transaction tools such as GCash and Maya which allow them to conduct secure and efficient business transactions.
